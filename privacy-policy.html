Privacy Policy – Star LPG

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Star LPG ("we", "us") operates the Star LPG mobile app for ordering LPG cylinders in Karachi, Pakistan. This policy explains what information the app collects and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Name and business name (optional for business) – to identify your order.
  • Phone number – to confirm your order, rate and delivery time.
  • Delivery address and area – to deliver your cylinder.
  • Precise location (optional) – only if you tap "Use my current location", so our rider can find you. It is never collected in the background.
  • Order details – cylinder size, quantity, order type, chosen branch and notes.

How we use it

Only to process, confirm and deliver your order and to contact you about it. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except our service provider below.

Where it is stored

Orders are stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore (Google LLC), which encrypts data in transit. Order data is accessed by Star LPG branch staff at Dalmia Road, Saadi Town and SITE / Orangi.

Retention and deletion

We keep order records as long as needed for delivery, customer service and our business records. To ask us to delete your information, call or WhatsApp 0300-8599961 and we will remove it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
